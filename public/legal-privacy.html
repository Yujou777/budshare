2. The short version

  • We collect what we need to run a private, 18+, growers-only swap network: your account details, what you list and post, your chats with swap partners, ratings and reports, and basic technical data.
  • Other logged-in members can see your profile, listings and posts. Only you and your chat partner can see your chats.
  • We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not send direct marketing.
  • We host with Render (Frankfurt, Germany, EU) and store data with Supabase (Zurich, Switzerland).
  • Age: we confirm you are 18+ from the date of birth you give us and your 18+ declaration.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, and you can complain to the Information Regulator.

3. Personal information we collect

3.1 Information you give us

Account details - display name and email address; - password, which we store only as a one-way bcrypt hash and never in readable form; - date of birth (to confirm you are 18+); - country, area/region and town/city.

Profile photo – optional.

Strain listings - strain name, type (Indica / Sativa / hybrid / 50-50), grow method (Indoor / Outdoor / Greenhouse), organic flag and description; - photos of the plant in flower, of the dried flower, and any extra photos; - an optional Certificate of Analysis (COA), which may itself contain names or other details.

Feed activity – posts (text and photos), comments and likes.

Swaps - swap proposals, counter-offers, acceptances and cancellations; - the strains and grams involved; - receipt confirmations ("Received" / "Not received").

Chat messages with your swap partners, including anything you choose to write, such as a delivery or collection address.

Courier details you share in chat (courier name, tracking number(s), notes), including the history of any updates.

Ratings you give and receive (flower and member stars, comments).

Reports you make or that are made about you (reason, note, the swap concerned, and the outcome).

Consents – your acceptance of the Terms and of the swap-at-your-own-risk notice, with the date.

Messages to us – for example support or privacy requests.

3.2 Age confirmation

Bud Share is for adults only. We confirm age by: - your date of birth, which is required at sign-up. Sign-up is refused if it shows you are under 18; and - your declaration, by ticking a box, that you are 18 or older.

Members who joined before date of birth was required are asked for it at their next login. If the date entered is under 18, the account is closed.

We do not ask for ID documents or photos to confirm your age.

3.3 Information collected automatically

Cookies. We use only the cookies needed to run the service:

Cookie Purpose Duration
bs (with signature) Keeps you logged in (session) Up to 30 days
bsgate Remembers that you passed the private-test access gate or used a valid invite link Up to 1 year

We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

Local storage on your device - the invite code you arrived with, until sign-up completes; - cached app files so the installable app (PWA) loads quickly.

Technical logs. Our hosting provider and our server keep standard logs, such as: - IP address; - date and time; - page or API path requested; - browser/device type; and - errors.

We use them for security, abuse prevention and fixing problems.

Read status – when you last read each chat, so we can show unread messages.

3.4 Information from other members

  • Ratings, reports and chat messages other members write about or to you.
  • Invite referrals: if you joined through someone's invite link, we record who invited you, and your profile shows "Invited by" that member. Members also see how many people they invited.

3.5 Cannabis-related information

Your use of Bud Share shows that you grow and use cannabis. Reports may contain allegations of unlawful conduct (for example selling). Under POPIA, information about alleged criminal behaviour is special personal information. We process it only where: - it is necessary to establish, exercise or defend a right or obligation in law (POPIA s27(1)(b)); or - you have consented; and - in all cases, only for safety, report handling and legal compliance.

Please do not include health information or more personal details than needed in posts or chats.

6. Cross-border transfers (POPIA section 72)

Our providers store and process data outside South Africa, in Germany (EU) and Switzerland. We transfer personal information there only because: - both are subject to data-protection law that provides an adequate level of protection, substantially similar to POPIA (the GDPR in the EU; the revised Federal Act on Data Protection in Switzerland); and - our providers are bound by data-processing terms; and - the transfer is necessary to perform our agreement with you (s72(1)(a) and (c)).

Some providers may also access data from other countries, such as the USA, for support or security. They do so under their own contractual safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

International members' information is processed in the same locations.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures (POPIA s19), including: - HTTPS encryption in transit; - passwords stored only as bcrypt hashes; - signed, HTTP-only session and access cookies; - a private access gate during testing; - noindex settings so search engines don't list the site; - restricted admin access; - encrypted (SSL) database connections; and - no secrets in public code.

No system is perfectly secure. If a security compromise is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA s22 requires.

Please: - protect your password; - don't share the site access code; - think carefully before posting an address or other identifying details in chat.

9. Children

Bud Share is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an under-18 person has joined, we will delete their account and information, except for a minimal record to prevent re-registration. Report suspected under-18 members using Report → Under 18, or email hello@budshare.co.za.

10. Your rights

Under POPIA (and the GDPR where it applies to you) you have the right to: - Access – ask whether we hold your information and receive a copy (POPIA s23). - Correction and deletion – ask us to correct, update or delete inaccurate, out-of-date or unlawfully held information (POPIA s24). You can edit your profile and location in the app at any time. - Object – object to processing based on legitimate interests (POPIA s11(3)). - Withdraw consent where we rely on it. - Not be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects without the chance to make representations (POPIA s71). - For GDPR-covered members, additionally – data portability, restriction of processing, and the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

To exercise your rights: - email hello@budshare.co.za from your registered email address; - we may ask you to verify your identity; - we will respond within a reasonable time, and generally within 30 days; - requests are free, except where the law allows a prescribed fee for access to records.
